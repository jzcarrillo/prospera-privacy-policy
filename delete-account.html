Account & Data Deletion

Request deletion of your Prospera account

Prospera users can request deletion of their account and associated personal and financial data by contacting our support team. Requests are reviewed and processed through our backend systems.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address registered with your Prospera account.
  2. Address it to prospera.appsupport@gmail.com.
  3. Use the subject line Prospera Account Deletion Request.
  4. Our support team will verify account ownership, process the deletion request, and communicate the outcome by email.

Request account deletion by email

What information is covered?

The request covers the account's registered email address and associated financial records stored for Prospera, including assets, budgets, daily and monthly expenses, income, loans, receivables, and savings.

Data retention

We will process deletion of the account and associated data, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements. We will explain any applicable exceptions when responding to your request.
