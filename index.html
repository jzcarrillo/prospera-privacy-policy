Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Prospera ("we", "us", or "our") is a personal finance application that helps users organize financial information and understand their financial health. This Privacy Policy explains what information users provide to Prospera, why we process it, and how users can contact us about their data.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, you provide your full name, email address, and password. Authentication credentials are handled through an authentication service. The confirm-password field is used to check your entry during registration. You may also enter financial information into the app, including:

  • Assets and their values
  • Budgets and budget details
  • Daily and monthly expenses
  • Income
  • Loans and repayment information
  • Receivables
  • Savings and savings goals

Financial records described above are information entered by users in the app. This policy does not claim that Prospera directly connects to bank accounts or retrieves banking transactions.

2. How We Use Information

We process account and financial information to register and authenticate users, operate the app, maintain users' records, calculate the Financial Health Score, and generate financial summaries and reports. We use the registered email address to support account-related requests. Users may also unlock the app using their device's built-in biometric authentication; Prospera does not receive or store facial images or biometric templates through this feature.

3. Storage and Service Providers

Prospera stores account information and user-entered financial records using cloud-based infrastructure operated with the assistance of hosting and data-processing service providers. We use this infrastructure to provide and maintain the app. We will update this policy to describe other relevant processing arrangements where applicable.

4. Data Security

We aim to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, and disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Details of specific technical controls will be described here when verified against the deployed implementation.

5. Data Sharing

Account and financial information is processed to provide Prospera’s functionality, including by infrastructure service providers acting on our behalf. We will update this policy to accurately disclose any additional sharing or processing arrangements, including analytics or other third-party services if used.

7. Financial Portfolio Sharing and User Consent

Prospera allows users to request access to a limited view of another registered user's financial portfolio, such as a spouse, partner, or trusted individual. Portfolio sharing is optional and requires the portfolio owner's explicit approval. A request alone does not grant access.

Information visible to approved users. Approved users can view only the portfolio owner's Financial Health Status Dashboard and Monthly Financial Summary. Detailed financial records and other modules, including assets, budgets, expenses, income, loans, receivables, and savings, are not directly accessible through this sharing feature.

Revoking access. Portfolio owners can revoke previously approved access at any time. After revocation, the other user is no longer authorized to view the shared dashboard or monthly summary through Prospera.

Financial portfolios are not publicly accessible through this feature. Access is limited to users approved by the portfolio owner.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Prospera account and associated personal and financial records by emailing prospera.appsupport@gmail.com from their registered email address, with the subject Prospera Account Deletion Request.

After verifying account ownership, our support team handles deletion requests manually, including the user's account and associated financial records. We will communicate the outcome to the requester. Any data required to be retained by applicable law may be handled separately.

See the Account Deletion Instructions for details.

8. Your Choices and Requests

For questions about your information, requests to access or correct records, or account deletion requests, contact prospera.appsupport@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on requests.

9. Children's Privacy

Prospera is a personal finance management app. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the situation.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
